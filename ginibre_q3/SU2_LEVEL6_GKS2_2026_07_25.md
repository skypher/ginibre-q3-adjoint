# Full `GKS2*` for `SU(2)_6`

Date: 2026-07-25

## Result

Every even-minus signed word of arbitrary length in the fusion ring
`SU(2)_6` has nonnegative vacuum corner, and adjoining one plus factor gives
nonnegativity of every partial-character coefficient:

```text
[V_0 tensor V_0]
 product_i(V_(a_i) tensor V_0
           +epsilon_i V_0 tensor V_(a_i)) >= 0
```

whenever the number of minus factors is even, for labels `a_i in {0,...,6}`.

This is the first even level beyond four, and the first new level of any
parity since eleven. The previously proved family `k <= 5, 7, 9, 11` becomes
`k <= 7, 9, 11`.

## 1. Reduction to a finite residual census

The standard overlap reduction removes any label occurring with both signs,
leaving support-disjoint chambers. The spectral model is the Verlinde
diagonalisation at `n = 8`:

```text
w_j = 2/8 sin^2((j+1)pi/8),
chi_a(j) = sin((a+1)(j+1)pi/8) / sin((j+1)pi/8),
```

validated end to end against exact integer fusion DP before use
(`verify_su2_level_spectral_dp.py`, 240 random words across levels 2–8,
worst relative error `1.8e-13`). Diagonal node pairs vanish on any minus
factor, and off-diagonal pairs symmetrise under even minus parity.

Fixing each active label's sign and minimum-exponent parity and splitting
every residual orthant by its vanishing coordinates gives exactly

```text
257,908 support/parity/residual regimes,
124,928 identically zero by the parity selection rule,
132,980 nonzero regimes to certify.
```

The parity exclusion is the proved selection rule of
`SU2_PARITY_SELECTION_RULE_2026_07_24.md`: the corner vanishes unless the
total degree `sum_i a_i p_a` is even, and residual increments preserve that
parity, so the excluded chambers are exactly zero rather than certified.

## 2. Certificate classes

Every one of the 132,980 nonzero regimes is closed by one of the following,
in a single run of the engine
(`character_ring_iter/verify_su2_orbit_amgm_stack.cpp`, `--level 6
--certify-all --decompose`):

```text
pointwise nonnegative chambers            70,644
capacitated AM-GM allocations             60,256
recursive orthant decompositions           2,080
  of which Farey merge sectors               286
  of which exact Abel chains                 125
  with exact integer fusion leaves           74
                                         -------
total                                    132,980      open: 0
```

1. **Pointwise chambers**: every spectral term positive, with term signs
   decided by 512-bit outward-rounded intervals, never by floating point.
2. **Capacitated AM-GM allocations**: a share of each positive term is
   allocated to each negative; weighted AM-GM with the allocation as its own
   weight vector reduces the regime to linear conditions. Proposals come
   from a linear program with greedy slack-optimal rational rounding and
   optimal-face vertex retries; nothing proposed is accepted unverified.
3. **Recursive orthant decomposition**: the exact split
   `{u=0} disjoint-union {u>=1}` in a chosen coordinate, sound by
   construction, with dimension-scaled depth.
4. **Farey merge sectors**: where the allocation polytope sits exactly on
   its feasibility boundary — competing rows crossing at an irrational
   weight, so that no single rational allocation exists on the whole
   orthant — the exponent cone is split `{u_a >= u_b}` against
   `{u_a <= u_b}`. Each half is a unimodular substitution merging the pair
   into one coordinate with base `lambda_a lambda_b`; one competing row goes
   slack in each half. This is the class the recorded `O_11` census called
   the Farey fan.
5. **Exact Abel chains**: terms grouped by exact lambda-vector equality in
   `Z[zeta]`; when the groups form an exact dominance chain, summation by
   parts certifies the regime for all exponents from exact prefix-sum
   nonnegativity.
6. **Exact integer fusion leaves**: zero-dimensional nodes evaluated as
   exact integers by fusion DP over GMP, so exact zeros certify as
   nonnegative. A strictly negative leaf would refute the conjecture and is
   reported as such; none occurred.

## 3. Trusted-computation boundary

Three spectral models are built independently — long double trigonometry,
512-bit MPFR intervals with directed rounding, and exact elements of
`Z[zeta]`, `zeta = 2cos(pi/8)`, with minimal polynomial `x^4 - 4x^2 + 2`
derived by integer cyclotomic division and back-substitution whose residual
must vanish identically. The models are cross-checked at startup and the run
aborts on any disagreement, including any sign disagreement on a term base.

Floating point proposes and never decides. Certificate rows are checked in
interval arithmetic; a row the interval cannot decide is settled by the sign
of a single element of `Z[zeta]`, determined at precision scaled to the
element's height, with exact zero passing because weighted AM-GM permits
equality. Structural conditions are checked exactly over the integers.

The run self-validates: the actual signed sum is evaluated at 80,000
pseudo-random exponent points across 2,000 sampled certified regimes with
zero violations, and 1,000 deliberately corrupted allocations are all
refused. The recursion's node budget is never the binding constraint.

## 6. Consequences and next targets

By the exact finite-to-stable transfer, the proved family `k <= 7, 9, 11`
covers every ordinary partial coefficient with
`max(max_i a_i, c, ceil((T+c)/2)) <= 7` and the levels nine and eleven
windows. The even-level barrier is broken: the same engine and certificate
classes apply verbatim to levels 8, 10 and 12, which are the next targets,
and to the orbit rings `O_11` and `O_13`, where the exact layers have not yet
been measured.

The 286 Farey-merge regimes and 125 Abel-chain regimes are the boundary
structure of this level: the tight and zero cases any uniform-in-`k`
induction must reproduce. They are recorded by the engine as a byproduct and
feed the factor-axis program, which remains the route to the full conjecture.
