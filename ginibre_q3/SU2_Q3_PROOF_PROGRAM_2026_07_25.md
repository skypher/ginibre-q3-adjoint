# The SU(2) Q3 program: goal, reduction, and machine strategy

Date: 2026-07-25

## Goal

Prove Ginibre's Q3 condition for the closed multiplicative convex cone
generated by **all** irreducible real SU(2) characters — preferably by
establishing `GKS2*` uniformly for the finite fusion rings `SU(2)_k` and
passing to the stable limit.

## The reduction chain, all proved

1. **Stable transfer** (`SU2_FINITE_STABLE_REDUCTION_2026_07_24.md`): for
   `k >=` the word's total label sum, the finite coefficient *equals* the
   ordinary one. So `GKS2*` for every finite `k` implies the goal, formally;
   the limit is not analytic.
2. **Odd lifting** (`SU2_ODD_SIMPLE_CURRENT_LIFT_2026_07_24.md`): odd level
   `GKS2*` is equivalent to the rank-`(k+1)/2` orbit ring.
3. **Parity rule** (`SU2_PARITY_SELECTION_RULE_2026_07_24.md`): odd total
   degree vanishes identically; only even-degree chambers are in play.
4. **Even-level reduction** (`SU2_EVEN_MIXED_TRACE_REDUCTION_2026_07_24.md`):
   the fixed-node correction is a finite integral fusion coefficient.
5. **Parity-separated arbitrary-factor chamber** (Proposition 20): when
   every minus label is odd and every plus label is even, the odd-label
   toggle turns the word into an all-plus word.  Hence `GKS2*` holds
   termwise in this chamber for every level and every factor count.
6. **Complete finite two-generator cone** (Corollary 24B17B):
   Propositions 24B17 and 24B17A prove full `GKS2*`, at arbitrary word
   length, for the cone generated by `V_1,V_2` in every even and odd
   `SU(2)_k`, respectively.  Propositions 24A--24B2 also give invariant
   arbitrary-length one-/two-minus suffix cones used by the wider program.

Level status: `k <= 7, 9, 11` proved — **`SU(2)_6` closed 2026-07-25**
(`SU2_LEVEL6_GKS2_2026_07_25.md`), the first new level since eleven and the
first even level beyond four. Levels 8, 10, 12 are the next targets with the
same engine. `O_13` at 84% machine coverage, exact layers not yet measured
there.

## What certificates can and cannot contribute

There are infinitely many levels, so no amount of per-level certification
proves the goal. The proof must come from a `k`-uniform argument. The two
lanes the repository's own record supports:

- **Factor axis**: all words of `<= 6` factors are proved in *every* `SU(2)_k`
  (the pair reservoir `T <= N + P`, Prop 25D4), and the ordinary seven-factor
  two-minus case is closed.  Corollary 5A1 now reduces the remaining
  four-/six-minus sign patterns to eight exact parity-orbit types, and Proposition
  5A2 closes the ordinary all-even four-minus orbit by the raw bound
  `T_- <= 16d <= N`.  Corollary 5A3 carries the same proof uniformly through
  the finite half-level box, while Proposition 5A4 closes every full-wall
  row with cap at least three and maximal cut `d >= 6`.  Proposition 5A5
  closes `d <= 5` by 160 cutoff-free QF-LIA queries, so Corollary 5A6 proves
  the complete finite cap-at-least-three stratum.  The fourteen-cell
  cutoff-free replay in Corollary 5A6A closes the complementary shallow
  rows and hence the complete finite all-even four-minus stratum.  Frontier:
  finish the deep rank-at-most-two two-minus replay (Corollary 23A9ZZ8
  now assembles every complementary rank-at-least-three case),
  close the other seven parity-orbit types with the active 56-cell replay,
  consolidate the uniform seven-factor theorem, and then prove the
  arbitrary-factor induction. Everything here is uniform in `k`, which is
  why it can finish.
- **Level base + data**: each newly closed level extends the proved family
  and, through the stable transfer, proves every ordinary word with
  `max(max a_i, ceil(T/2)) <= k`. Equally important, the engine's failures
  are data: the tight and zero cases are exactly what a uniform induction
  must respect.

## The engine now runs on any level directly

The full-ring Verlinde spectrum has the same two-node structure as the orbit
rings — diagonals vanish on any minus factor, off-diagonal pairs symmetrise
under even minus parity — so the certificate machinery applies to any level,
even or odd, with no orbit reduction. `--level k` selects it.

The spectrum was validated before use: 240 random words across levels 2–8
against exact integer fusion DP (`verify_su2_level_spectral_dp.py`), worst
relative error `1.8e-13`, including odd-degree words vanishing exactly.

Full-level spectra introduce two things the orbit rings never had, both now
handled:

- **Interior character zeros** (`chi_a(j) = 0` when `(a+1)(j+1) | k+2`): the
  float model computes these as `sin(m*pi) ~ 1e-19`, not zero, so the
  degenerate-base test compares both arithmetics at roundoff scale and aborts
  on real disagreement. Term signs are now interval-authoritative with a
  float-agreement abort.
- **Exact zeros of the corner itself**: no strict interval test certifies an
  exact zero. Zero-dimensional leaves are now decided by **exact integer
  fusion DP** over GMP (`leaf_exact_nonneg`), the same device as the published
  works' integral fusion leaves. A strictly negative exact leaf would refute
  the conjecture and is reported as such.

## Validation against known theorems

With exact leaves, `--certify-all` (no tolerance-based Hall shortcut):

```text
O_7   204 / 204     100.0%   soundness 8,160 pts, 0 violations
O_9 2,408 / 2,408   100.0%   soundness 96,320 pts, 0 violations
```

Both rings correspond to proved theorems, so this is ground-truth validation
of the entire pipeline, twice. Two validation-harness defects surfaced and
were fixed along the way: the capacity negative-control is only a genuine
corruption when at least two negatives compete, and the soundness sampler's
tolerance must scale with the cancelling mass rather than the result, because
exact-zero points — newly certified, hence newly sampled — cancel
catastrophically in long double.

Proved-level calibration of the full-level mode: level 2 closes 100%, levels
3–4 reach 93/89% with the remainder being **exact ties** — domination rows
holding with exact equality, which strict interval arithmetic honestly cannot
decide.

## The exact-tie engine layer

Exact ties need exact algebra: the spectral values live in
`Q(2cos(pi/(k+2)))`, and deciding a tied row means recognising equality
there.  The cyclotomic layer now constructs the exact minimal polynomial,
reduces every tied row in `Z[2cos(pi/(k+2))]`, and evaluates the sign of a
nonzero residue over an outward MPFR enclosure of the distinguished root.
The root enclosure was corrected and independently tested on 2026-07-26; the
corrected level-six replay again closed all 132,980 nonzero regimes.  The
source hashes and terminal status are in `certificates/su2_level6_amgm.log`,
and the 6,000-element exact-sign regression is in
`certificates/su2_cyclotomic_sign.log`.

## The level-6 residue is Galois-structured

The 137 regimes surviving every certificate class at level 6 have a clean
characterisation, established by measurement rather than guess:

- every one is LP-feasible, with the optimum at a zero-margin vertex whose
  tie structure rational rounding cannot reproduce at any denominator, and
  widening the vertex search (jitter 6 to 24) recovers nothing further;
- probing a survivor exactly shows its terms pair perfectly under the Galois
  automorphism `zeta -> -zeta` of `Q(2cos(pi/8))`, and every conjugate pair's
  lambda product lands in the fixed subfield -- values like 2 and 4 are
  rational outright, the rest live in `Q(sqrt 2)`.
